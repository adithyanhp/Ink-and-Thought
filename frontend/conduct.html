Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Ink & Thought project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expectations & Norms

Our Standards

check_circle Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

  • task_alt Using welcoming and inclusive language.
  • task_alt Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • task_alt Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
  • task_alt Focusing on what is best for the community.
  • task_alt Showing empathy towards other community members.

error Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

  • block The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
  • block Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
  • block Public or private harassment.
  • block Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.
  • block Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

shield Enforcement

lock Confidential Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the platform administrators at conduct@inkandthought.io. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The community team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
